- MECHANICS -

This educational board game combines the classic Snakes and Ladders with ISO 7010 Safety Signs. Players must answer questions based on safety sign categories to earn their way up ladders and avoid sliding down snakes. The game promotes knowledge of internationally recognized safety signs while making learning enjoyable and interactive.

EQUIPMENT OF THE GAME

  • • 1 Folding game board
  • • 1 Dot-numbered die
  • • 6 Colored tokens
  • • Printed Game Rules
  • • 5 Decks of Question Cards
  • • Prohibition
  • • Warning
  • • Mandatory
  • • Fire Protection
  • • Safe Condition

SETTING UP THE GAME

A-01. NUMBER OF PLAYERS

• The game may be played by two to six players.

A-02. ORDER OF PLAY

• Each Player rolls the die once.

• The player with the highest roll goes first.

• Play continues clockwise.

A-03. DISTRIBUTION OF TOKENS

• Each player selects one token and places it ouside the board

• Shuffle all fice decks separately and place them beside the board according to their category

PLAYING THE GAME

B-01. START OF THE GAME
  1. • The first player rolls the die and moves forward according to the number rolled.
  2. • Turn continue clockwise

B-02. LANDING ON A REGULAR SPACE
  1. • If a player lands on a space without a snake or ladder, no question is asked.
  2. • The player's turn ends immediately.

B-03. LANDING ON A LADDER SPACE
  1. • If a player lands on the base of a ladder, the player must:
  2. • Draw one (1) question card from the deck corresponding to the color of the space landed on
  3. • Answer the question printed on the card.
  4. • If the answer is correct, the player climbs the ladder to the connected space.
  5. • If the player is incorrect or is unable toanswer, the player remains on the current space.
  6. • The turn then ends.
B-04. LANDING ON A SNAKE SPACE
  1. • If a player lands on the head of a snake, the player must:
  2. • Draw one (1) question card from the deck corresponding to the color of the color of the space landed on.
  3. • Answer the question printed on the card.
  4. • If the answer is correct, the player remains and does not slide down the snake.
  5. • If the player is incorrect or is unable to answer, the player slides down the snake to its tail.
  6. • The turn ends.
B-05. QUESTION CARDS
  1. • Only one question card is drawn for each snake or ladder space landed on.
  2. • After answering, the card is returned to the bottom if tis respective deck
B-06. WINNING THE GAME
  1. • If an exact roll is required, players must roll the exact number needed to land on the Finish Space.
  2. • The first player to reach the Finish Space is declared the winner.
